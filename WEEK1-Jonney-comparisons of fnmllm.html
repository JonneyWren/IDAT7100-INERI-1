01

Concept & Scope

Conversational quant generation converts investment ideas into formal strategies that can be validated, backtested and potentially deployed.

Input

Natural-language investment objectives, preferences and constraints.

Core process

Translate intent into explicit, machine-readable strategy logic.

Principle

Language is the interface—not the strategy. Validation and controlled execution remain essential.

02

Competitive Landscape

The platforms address different stages of the quantitative investment workflow.

Capitalise.ai · Rule generation

Natural language to explicit trading conditions.

Composer · Portfolio strategy

Investment objectives to portfolio structure and allocation rules.

QuantConnect · Quant development

Algorithm coding, backtesting and deployment.

TrendSpider · Technical analysis

Chart analysis, signal research and strategy testing.

Bloomberg BQuant · Research

Python-oriented institutional data and analytics environment.

Comparison

They are complementary categories, not direct substitutes.

03

Three Generation Patterns

The main difference is the form of strategy representation produced by the system.

Natural Language → Rules

Conditions and actions. Best for explicit, relatively simple trading instructions.

Natural Language → Strategy

Assets, weights and portfolio logic. Best for allocation-level investment ideas.

Natural Language → Code

Executable Python/C#. Best for flexible research, algorithm development and iteration.

05

Proposed System Architecture

A modular pipeline separates language understanding from execution and independent validation.

1 · Understand

Extract objectives, assets, horizon and constraints.

2 · Formalize

Create a structured strategy schema.

3 · Generate

Produce strategy logic and executable code.

4 · Validate

Check schema, code, security and risk limits.

5 · Backtest

Run sandbox tests with fees and slippage.

6 · Monitor

Track performance, risk, token cost and latency.

Design choice: Use a structured strategy schema as the interface between the LLM, validators and backtesting engine.
06

Strategy Schema

A machine-readable specification makes strategy logic inspectable, testable and reusable.

Universe & signals

Assets, indicators, entry and exit conditions.

Parameters & constraints

Lookback, rebalance frequency, position limits and drawdown limits.

Cost budget

Trading-cost assumptions and LLM token/inference budget.

07

Dynamic Cost-Aware Optimization

Each additional AI optimization cycle should justify its inference cost and risk implications.

Objective

J = αP − βCtrade − γCLLM − δR

Performance & costs

P: strategy performance. Ctrade: fees, spread and slippage.

AI cost & risk

CLLM: inference cost. R: risk penalty.

Illustrative objective: normalize terms before combining them and enforce critical risk limits as hard constraints.
08

Validation & Research Priorities

Evaluate both technical reliability and the quality of the resulting strategy.

Validation

Intent accuracy, schema correctness, code checks and reproducible backtests.

Robustness

Realistic trading costs, out-of-sample tests and risk-limit enforcement.

Research questions

Schema-first or direct code? Which parameters can self-optimize? When is another AI iteration worthwhile?

09

Takeaway

A practical design combines strategy formalization, algorithm development and cost-aware feedback.

Strategy layer

Composer-style structured portfolio and strategy representation.

Engineering layer

QuantConnect-style development, validation and reproducible backtesting.

Research contribution

Dynamic AI iteration balancing performance, trading costs, risk and inference cost.
